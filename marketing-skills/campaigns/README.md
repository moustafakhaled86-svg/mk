# Campaign Management Skills

Planning, execution, and optimization of marketing campaigns.

## Campaign Planning

### Pre-Launch Phase

1. **Define Objectives**
   - SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
   - KPIs to track
   - Success criteria

2. **Audience Analysis**
   - Target segments
   - Buyer personas
   - Audience size
   - Competitive landscape

3. **Budget Allocation**
   - Total budget
   - Channel breakdown
   - Reserve budget for optimization
   - Contingency fund

4. **Message & Creative**
   - Key messaging
   - Creative concepts
   - Variations (A/B test candidates)
   - Brand alignment

5. **Channel Strategy**
   - Primary channels
   - Secondary channels
   - Timing
   - Frequency

## Testing & Optimization

### A/B Testing
- Subject lines
- Headlines
- Images
- CTAs
- Landing pages
- Ad copy

### Multivariate Testing
- Multiple elements simultaneously
- Larger sample sizes
- Statistical significance

### Optimization Cycle
1. Identify hypothesis
2. Design test
3. Run experiment
4. Analyze results
5. Implement winner
6. Repeat

## Performance Monitoring

### Daily Tasks
- Check delivery rates
- Monitor early engagement
- Watch for technical issues
- Respond to alerts

### Weekly Review
- Performance against targets
- Identify top performers
- Quick optimizations
- Team sync-up

### Post-Campaign Analysis

1. **Metrics Summary**
   - Reach & impressions
   - Engagement rates
   - Conversion metrics
   - ROI calculation

2. **Learnings**
   - What worked
   - What didn't
   - Surprises/insights
   - Competitor activity impact

3. **Recommendations**
   - Quick wins to repeat
   - Process improvements
   - Strategic adjustments
   - Budget reallocation

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. Not setting clear objectives
2. Insufficient testing time
3. Over-targeting audience
4. Poor creative quality
5. Ignoring mobile optimization
6. Not tracking all conversions
7. Insufficient budget
8. No contingency planning
